Dax runs from your menu bar and stays out of the way until you press a
shortcut. This guide walks you through everything from download to your
first result.

## What you need

- A Mac running macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later
- Apple Silicon or Intel — both work

## Step 1 — Download and install

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download Dax">
    Head to [getdax.app/download](https://getdax.app/download/). The
    installer starts downloading automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the installer">
    Open the downloaded `.pkg` file and follow the prompts. Dax lands in
    your Applications folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Dax">
    Launch Dax from Applications. You'll see a small icon appear in your
    **menu bar** at the top of the screen — that's home base.

    <Tip>
      If macOS asks "Are you sure you want to open this?" — click **Open**.
      Dax is signed and notarized by Apple.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2 — Set up your account

The first time you open Dax, a setup screen appears. You have two options:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Bring your own key (BYOK)" icon="key">
    Already have an [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai) API key? Paste it
    in and you're done — AI tools use your key directly. No quotas, no
    middleman.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sign up for a Dax account" icon="user-plus">
    Enter your name and email. We'll send you a 4-character code — type it
    in and your account activates instantly. Start on the Free plan and
    upgrade to Pro any time.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  **Already have an invite code?** Tap "Already have a code?" on the setup
  screen and enter it directly.
</Note>

## Step 3 — Try your first tool

Let's turn an image into a shareable link with [Image to URL](/tools/image-to-url).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy an image">
    Take a screenshot (<kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>⇧</kbd><kbd>4</kbd>) or copy any
    image to your clipboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press the shortcut">
    Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>U</kbd> from anywhere. That's
    **Control + Option + U**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get the link">
    Dax uploads the image and copies a shareable URL to your clipboard.
    Paste it wherever you need it — a chat, a pull request, a document.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Not sure about shortcuts?** Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd>
  <kbd>Space</kbd> (Control + Option + Space) to open the **launcher grid**
  — every tool is one click away. You can also click the Dax icon in your
  menu bar.
</Tip>

<Note>
  **What's a shortcut?** It's a key combination you press to instantly open
  a tool. In Dax, most shortcuts are **Control + Option + a letter**. You
  hold Control and Option together, then tap the letter. For example,
  <kbd>⌃</kbd><kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>K</kbd> means hold Control + Option, then
  press K.
</Note>
