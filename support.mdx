Need help with Dax? Here is how to reach the team and find answers.

## In-app support

The fastest way to get help is right from Dax itself.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Feedback" icon="message-dots" href="/tools/feedback">
    Report a bug, ask a question, or send a note. Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>B</kbd> from any app.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Suggest a Tool" icon="lightbulb" href="/tools/suggest-a-tool">
    Have an idea for something Dax should do? Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>F</kbd>.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Both tools send directly to the Dax team. For bug reports, attach a screen snip and include what you expected to happen.
