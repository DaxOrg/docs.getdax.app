Need help with Dax? Here is how to reach the team and find answers.

## In-app support

The fastest way to get help is right from Dax itself.

<Card title="Feedback" icon="message-dots" href="/accelerators/feedback">
  Report a bug, ask a question, suggest a feature, or send a note. Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>B</kbd> from any app.
</Card>

Feedback sends directly to the Dax team. For bug reports, attach a screen snip and include what you expected to happen.

## Community

If something feels off, check [status.getdax.app](https://status.getdax.app) for current system status and incident history.
