Accelerators are built into Dax. Each one does a single job you'd otherwise
leave your work for: turning a screenshot into a link, asking a quick
question, handing a secret to an agent, recording a clip. They open over
whatever app you're in, do the job, and get out of the way.

They're part of the same platform as the [main window](/main-window), where
your coding agents run, and [Dax Companion](/companion) on your phone. Many
are there for your agents too: an agent in Dax can put files on your
[Shelf](/accelerators/shelf), mark up your screen, or ask you for a
[secret](/accelerators/secrets) without ever seeing its value.

## Open one

- **Its shortcut.** Every Accelerator has a global shortcut, <kbd>⌃</kbd>
  <kbd>⌥</kbd> plus one key by default. The [shortcuts page](/shortcuts)
  lists them all.
- **The launcher.** Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>Space</kbd> for a
  searchable grid of every Accelerator. Click one, or press
  <kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>9</kbd> for the first nine.
- **The menu bar.** Click the Dax icon to find them there too.

## Make them yours

- **Switch one off** in **Settings → Accelerators** (<kbd>⌃</kbd>
  <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd>). An Accelerator that's off doesn't answer its
  shortcut and leaves the launcher.
- **Rebind its shortcut** in **Settings → Shortcuts** (<kbd>⌃</kbd>
  <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>H</kbd>).
- **Arrange the launcher.** Choose **Arrange Accelerators…** to drag them
  into your own order and pin the ones you use most, or sort by most used,
  recently used, or A–Z.

## Build your own

Write a custom Accelerator in JavaScript and Dax draws it with native macOS
controls. It opens from the launcher like the built-in ones, and you can give
it its own shortcut. See the [Accelerator SDK](/developers/accelerator-sdk).

<Note>
  Custom Accelerators work in Dax downloaded from getdax.app. The Setapp
  version of Dax runs the built-in ones only.
</Note>
