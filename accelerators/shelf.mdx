The **Shelf** slides out of one edge of your screen and holds the files you
want on hand. Drop files in, then drag them back out into Finder, Slack, a
browser or a Dax pane. It keeps references, never copies, so the Shelf takes
no space.

## Open it

Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> from any app. Press it again, or
<kbd>Esc</kbd>, to put it away.

To open it from a different edge for one showing, press <kbd>⌃</kbd>
<kbd>⌥</kbd> and an arrow. The Shelf travels the way the arrow points:
<kbd>↑</kbd> comes up from the bottom, <kbd>↓</kbd> drops from the top. The
edge in Settings stays the same.

Opening the Shelf never takes the keyboard from the app you're typing in.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Drop files in">
    Drag files or folders onto the Shelf. Only the path is kept. A rename or
    a move doesn't lose the file. Text and pictures dropped without a file
    behind them are saved as clips.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at one">
    Click a file to select it. Press <kbd>Space</kbd> to Quick Look it, and
    double-click it or press <kbd>Return</kbd> to open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag it out">
    Drag a file into Finder, Slack, a browser or a Dax pane. Check several
    tiles (or <kbd>⌘</kbd>-click them) to drag them out together.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You don't have to press the shortcut to drop something. While you drag a
  file or a picture, a glowing sliver of the Shelf peeks out at its edge.
  Drop on it and the Shelf opens.
</Tip>

Pull the handle on the Shelf's inner edge to make it deeper or shallower.
Push it all the way back to close it.

## Tabs

- **Now** clears when Dax quits.
- Press **+** to make a tab of your own. Tabs you make are saved.
- **Clear** empties a tab. Deleting a tab never deletes the files.

A file deleted or moved away stays on the Shelf, marked in red, until you
remove it. Press **Remove deleted** to clear them all.

Right-click a file for **Open**, **Quick Look**, **Show in Finder**,
**Copy**, **Copy Path**, **Share…** and **Remove from Drawer**.

## Files, Notes and Finder

The switch in the Shelf's header has three sides:

- **Files** — your tabs of kept files, as a grid or a list.
- **Text** — notes you type or dictate, saved between launches. Copy one
  with its copy button.
- **Finder** — browse your Mac and drag files straight out. Drop one on a
  tab to keep it.

## Send to your iPhone or iPad

With a paired [Companion](/companion), right-click a file and choose
**Send to** your device. Check several tiles and press the **Send** bar to
send them as one bundle: one notification and one row on the phone, with
**Save all**.

Files you send from your phone land in the Shelf's **iPhone** tab. The Shelf
opens from the bottom with them checked, ready to drag. Text you send
becomes a note.

## Settings

Find these in **Settings → Accelerators → Shelf**:

- **Edge** — top, bottom, left or right. Top is the default.
- **Layout** — grid or list.
- **Peek when I drag a file** — on by default.

## For agents

Coding agents reach the Shelf through the `dax-drawer` MCP server. An agent
can put the files it made on the Shelf for you to drag out, see what you set
aside for it, manage tabs, and send a finished file to your phone when you
ask. Agents see names, paths and sizes, never file contents.
