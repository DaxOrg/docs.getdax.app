**Screen Markup** lets you draw straight on your screen: arrows, boxes,
highlights, labels, rulers and real blur. Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Return</kbd>
and the annotated screenshot lands in a card, uploaded and ready to share.

## Open it

Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>M</kbd> from any app.

Screen Markup needs **Screen Recording** permission. Dax asks for it the
first time.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Draw">
    The screen is captured and a toolbar appears at the top. Pick a tool and
    draw. Drag the toolbar's grip to move it out of the way.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish">
    Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Return</kbd>, or click **Finish**. Press
    <kbd>Esc</kbd> to cancel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    The result appears as a card in the corner. Drag it anywhere, copy its
    link once the upload is done, or reopen it to mark it up again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Frozen or live

- **Frozen** holds the picture still while you draw.
- **Live** keeps the picture updating underneath your marks.

Apps keep running in both. Switch with the **Frozen** / **Live** button in
the toolbar.

| Select | <kbd>V</kbd> |
| Pen | <kbd>P</kbd> |
| Highlighter | <kbd>H</kbd> |

| Blur | <kbd>R</kbd> |
| Label | <kbd>T</kbd> |
| Ruler | <kbd>M</kbd> |
| Undo / redo | <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> / <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> |

| Cancel | <kbd>Esc</kbd> |

Pick a colour and a stroke thickness (thin, medium or thick) from the
toolbar.

## Settings

Find these in **Settings → Accelerators → Screen Markup**:

- **Start in** — frozen or live screen.
- **Display** — the display under the pointer, the main display, or a
  specific one.
- **Starting drawing tool** — the tool selected when you open it.
- **Upload the annotated screenshot** — on by default. Uploads use your
  Storage settings.
- **Keep a copy in Pictures › Dax Shots › Screen Markup** — on by default.
- **Copy the image immediately** — off by default.

**Show all results** lists every markup, with **Retry upload**, **Copy
link** and **Remove**. Results are kept on your Mac. An upload cut off by a
relaunch waits for you to retry it.

## For agents

Coding agents use the `dax-screen-markup` MCP server to point at things on
your live screen, capture the screen with marks drawn in, or open Screen
Markup with their marks placed and wait for yours. That last one is how an
agent asks you to "show me where".
