**Vibe History** is a palette of your recent Claude Code and Codex
conversations, read straight from their trace files on disk. Pick one and
jump back in.

  <img src="/images/screenshots/vibe-history.jpg" alt="Vibe History palette" />
</Frame>

## Open it

Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> from any app.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the palette">
    Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd>. Your recent agent
    conversations float up as a list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick an action">
    Press <kbd>⇥</kbd> to cycle between three buttons across the top:

    - **Copy Resume Command** — puts `claude --resume …` on your clipboard.
    - **Open in Terminal** — opens your terminal in that project's folder
      with the resume command already running.
    - **Copy Context** — puts the whole conversation on the clipboard, to
      paste into a fresh agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Jump back in">
    Press <kbd>↩</kbd> on the conversation you want. <kbd>⌘</kbd>
    <kbd>C</kbd> copies the resume command from any of the three modes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Options

Settings → Tools → Vibe History:

- Toggle which agents to include: **Claude Code**, **Codex**.
- **Open sessions in** — which terminal app **Open in Terminal** uses.
  *Default* is whichever app your Mac already opens terminal scripts with;
  only terminals installed on this Mac are listed.

## Privacy

Local. It reads the trace files the agents already write on your Mac;
nothing is sent anywhere.
