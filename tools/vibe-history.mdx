**Vibe History** is your record of what you've made with Dax — prompts, answers,
generated images, links. When you need that thing from earlier, it's here
instead of gone.

## Open it

Choose **Vibe History** from the Dax menu bar.

## Use it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your history">
    Choose Vibe History from the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the entry">
    Scroll or search for what you're after.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reuse it">
    Copy it back out and use it again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Generated a link or a prompt earlier and lost it? Check Vibe History before
  redoing the work.
</Tip>

## Great for

- Recovering a link, prompt, or answer from earlier
- Reusing something you generated without regenerating it
- Seeing what you've been working on across tools
