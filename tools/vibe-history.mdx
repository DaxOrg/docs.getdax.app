**Vibe History** is a palette of your recent Claude Code and Codex
conversations, read straight from their trace files on disk — pick one and
jump back in.

## Open it

Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> from any app.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the palette">
    Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd>. Your recent agent
    conversations float up as a list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a conversation">
    Choose the one you want to resume.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Jump back in">
    The **resume command** for that conversation is **typed into the
    terminal you were in**, so you're right back where you left off.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Options

Settings → Tools → Vibe History:

- Toggle which agents to include: **Claude Code**, **Codex**.

## Privacy

Local. It reads the trace files the agents already write on your Mac;
nothing is sent anywhere.
