**Ask AI** (internally, *QuickQuestion*) is a floating prompt box for a quick
question, a lookup, or a small task, without switching windows or opening a
browser.

## Open it

Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> from any app.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the panel">
    Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> anywhere. An Ask AI panel
    floats up **without stealing focus** from your current window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask">
    Type anything (a question, a quick task, a lookup) and press
    <kbd>↩</kbd>.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the answer">
    A short answer appears in place, ready to copy with one click.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Keep questions short and specific. Ask AI is built for the quick "what's
  the flag for…" moments, not long research sessions.
</Tip>

## Privacy

Only the text you type is sent, and only when you press <kbd>↩</kbd>.

<Note>
  Working with an image instead of text? Use [Image Ask](/tools/image-ask).
</Note>
