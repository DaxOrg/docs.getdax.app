**Image Ask** lets you ask a question about anything on your screen. Draw a
box around it and get an answer in place, with follow-ups.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screenshots/image-ask.jpg" alt="Image Ask tool" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 12px;" />
</Frame>

## Open it

Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>I</kbd> from any app.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capture a region">
    Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>I</kbd> and **drag a selection**
    over any part of the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask about it">
    A panel opens. Ask a question about the captured area. The answer
    appears in place.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask follow-ups">
    Keep typing to ask follow-ups about the same capture. The conversation
    continues, fully keyboard-driven.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Screenshot a stack trace or a full error dialog and ask "what's causing
  this?", without retyping the message.
</Tip>

## Panel shortcuts

- <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>C</kbd>: copy the latest answer.
- <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>N</kbd>: start over with a new capture.

## Dax Shots

Every capture is saved to `~/Pictures/Dax Shots/Image Ask`, named the way macOS
names screenshots, so you can always find the image you asked about.

## Privacy

Captures are sent **only to answer your question** and are **not stored** on our
servers.

<Note>
  Just need a link to the image instead? Use [Image to URL](/tools/image-to-url).
  Want a prompt that recreates it? Use [Image to Prompt](/tools/image-to-prompt).
</Note>
