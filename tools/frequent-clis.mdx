**Frequent CLIs** is a quick launcher for the command-line tools you reach for
most — Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, and the rest of your kit. Press one
shortcut, pick a CLI, and go.

## Open it

Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>↩</kbd> from any app, or choose **Frequent
CLIs** from the Dax menu.

## Use it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the launcher">
    Press the shortcut to bring up your CLIs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick one">
    Choose the CLI you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Jump in">
    Dax takes you straight there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  This is the fastest way back into your AI coding tool mid-flow — no hunting
  through terminal tabs.
</Tip>

## Great for

- Bouncing between Claude Code, Codex, and other CLIs
- Getting back into your agent without breaking focus
- Keeping your most-used tools one keystroke away
