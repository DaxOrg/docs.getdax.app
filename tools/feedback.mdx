**Feedback** is a direct line to the team. Hit a bug, something feels off, or a
tool could work better — send a note without leaving the app.

## Open it

Choose **Feedback** from the Dax menu bar.

## Use it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Feedback">
    Choose it from the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your note">
    Tell us what happened or what could be better.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Submit — it reaches the team directly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  For bugs, a quick note on what you did and what you expected helps us fix it
  faster. A screenshot via [Image to URL](/tools/image-to-url) never hurts.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Got an idea for a brand-new tool? Use [Suggest a Tool](/tools/suggest-a-tool).
</Note>
