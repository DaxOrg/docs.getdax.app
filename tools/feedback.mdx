**Feedback** reports a bug, shares feedback, or asks a question, with
screenshots attached.

## Open it

Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>B</kbd> from any app.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the panel">
    Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>B</kbd>.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your report">
    Write your **bug report, feedback, or question**. Attach **screen snips**
    (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd>) to show exactly what you saw.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>↩</kbd> to send it to the feedback inbox.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  For bugs, a quick note on what you did and what you expected helps us fix
  it faster.
</Tip>

## Privacy

Only your words, the snips you choose, and the app / macOS version are sent.
nothing else.

<Note>
  Got an idea for a new feature or tool? Send it through Feedback — the team reads every message.
</Note>
