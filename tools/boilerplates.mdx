**Boilerplates** scaffolds a fresh project from a template in one motion, no
cloning, no find-and-replace. Works fully offline.

## Open it

Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>N</kbd> from any app.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the picker">
    Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>N</kbd>. Your templates float up as
    tiles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your project">
    Pick a template, name the project, and press <kbd>↩</kbd>.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start building">
    The folder is scaffolded into your **Workspace directory**, with every
    occurrence of `__PROJECT_NAME__` (in file names *and* contents) replaced
    by your project's name. It's ready to open in your editor with your
    [Open Project](/tools/open-project) command.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Templates

- Three starters ship: **iOS**, **Android**, **Vite React**.
- **Any folder** you drop into the templates directory becomes a template
  too. A template is just a folder of files; `__PROJECT_NAME__` is the only
  magic token. Manage them (add / reveal in Finder / remove) in Settings →
  Tools → Boilerplates.

## Download more templates

Browse the **[Boilerplates Marketplace](https://boilerplates.getdax.app/)**
for additional templates made by the Dax team. Each download is a `.daxel`
file. Double-click it and Dax installs the template automatically. It
shows up in your Boilerplates picker the next time you press
<kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>N</kbd>.
