**File Drop** (internally, *BulkUpload*) turns any files or folders into a
single shareable link — the fast way to hand a bundle of context to an LLM.

## Open it

Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>L</kbd> from any app.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the drop window">
    Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>L</kbd>. A drop window floats up and
    **stays up while you drag from Finder**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drop your files">
    A **single file** of any kind uploads **as-is**. **Multiple files or a
    folder** are bundled into **one zip**, then uploaded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload">
    Click **Upload**. The **link lands on your clipboard** — paste it to any
    LLM.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Select several files in Finder and drag them in together — File Drop keeps
  them as one batch.
</Tip>

## Limits & notes

- Up to **256 MB** per upload.
- Links **expire automatically after 90 days**.
- **Files only leave this Mac when you click Upload** — never on drop.
