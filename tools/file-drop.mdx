**File Drop** turns any files or folders into a single shareable link, the
fast way to hand a bundle of context to an LLM.

  <img src="/images/screenshots/file-drop.jpg" alt="File Drop — drag and drop zone" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 12px;" />
</Frame>

## Open it

Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>L</kbd> from any app. Or skip the
shortcut: **shake a file** you're already dragging and a drop shelf appears
under the pointer.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the drop window">
    Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>L</kbd>. A drop window floats up and
    **stays up while you drag from Finder**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drop your files">
    A **single file** of any kind uploads **as-is**. **Multiple files or a
    folder** are bundled into **one zip**, then uploaded.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/screenshots/file-drop-upload.jpg" alt="File Upload — drop files or folders here" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 12px;" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload">
    Click **Upload**. The **link lands on your clipboard**. Paste it to any
    LLM.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Shake to drop

Pick up a file anywhere, **wiggle it** while still holding, and a dark drop
shelf appears under the pointer. Drop on it and the upload starts immediately
— no <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>L</kbd> needed.

- **On by default**, with a one-time explanatory toast
- Switch it off in **Settings → Tools → File Drop → Shake to drop**
- Three quick horizontal direction reversals trigger the shelf

<Tip>
  Select several files in Finder and drag them in together. File Drop keeps
  them as one batch.
</Tip>

## Limits & notes

- Up to **256 MB** per upload.
- Links **expire automatically after 90 days**.
- **Files only leave this Mac when you click Upload**, never on drop.
- Closing the window no longer cancels the upload — it continues in the
  background.
