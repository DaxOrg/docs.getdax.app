**Clip to Link** records a part of your screen and gives you a shareable link.
Draw a box, hit record, and the link is on your clipboard before you can
switch apps.

## Open it

Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>;</kbd> from any app.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Draw a region">
    A crosshair appears. Drag a box around the part of the screen you want to
    record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record">
    Recording starts immediately. Click or press the shortcut again to stop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get the link">
    The clip uploads and the shareable link lands on your clipboard. Paste it
    anywhere.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Format

Choose between **GIF**, **MP4**, and **MOV**. The format you used last is the
one Dax offers next.

- **GIF:** plays everywhere, no player needed. Best for short UI demos.
- **MP4:** smaller files, better quality. Good for longer recordings.
- **MOV:** highest quality. Includes sound.

## Frame rate

15 and 30 fps are available. Frames divide the capture rate evenly so none are
dropped unevenly.

## What makes it different

- **GIFs move at the speed they were recorded.** Frames carry their original
  timestamps, so a pause is a pause and a fast bit is fast.
- **Clips have sound** (MOV format).
- **MOV shows its size** before you send it.
- **A format you've uploaded keeps its link.** Re-recording doesn't lose the
  previous clip.

## Privacy

The recording happens on your Mac. The clip is uploaded to get a shareable
link, using the same infrastructure as Image to URL and File Drop.
