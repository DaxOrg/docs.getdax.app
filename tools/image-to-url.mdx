**Image to URL** turns any image on your clipboard into a shareable link you
can paste anywhere — into a chat, an email, a document, or an AI conversation.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screenshots/image-to-url.jpg" alt="Image to URL — shareable link from any image" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 12px;" />
</Frame>

## Open it

Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>U</kbd> from any app.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy an image">
    Copy an image, or set the image source to a screen region instead (see
    Options below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press the shortcut">
    Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>U</kbd>. The image is uploaded to the
    image service.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the link">
    The returned link is **copied to your clipboard** and shown in a toast.
    Paste it anywhere.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Options

Settings → Tools → Image to URL:

- **Image source:** *Get Image from Clipboard* (default) or *Get Image from
  Screenshot* (draw a region on screen when you press the shortcut).

## Dax Shots

Every image you hand to this tool is saved to `~/Pictures/Dax Shots/Image to URL`,
named the way macOS names screenshots. The link expires after 90 days, but your
original is always there to find again.

## Notes

Links **expire automatically after 90 days**. The image only leaves your Mac
on the keypress. There's no background clipboard watching.
