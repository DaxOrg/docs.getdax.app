**AI Text Transform** rewrites selected text anywhere — improve it, shorten
it, fix its grammar, or apply your own instruction — and puts the result
right back.

## Open it

Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>\\</kbd> from any app.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select text">
    Select text in any app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press the shortcut">
    Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>\\</kbd>. A small menu of
    transformations appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a transformation">
    Pick one (or **Custom Prompt** to write your own instruction). The
    rewrite either **replaces your selection** or **lands on your
    clipboard**, depending on your setting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Built-in transformations

- **Write this better** (improve)
- **Enhance Prompt** — sharpen a prompt for an AI model
- **Shorten Text**
- **Fix grammar**
- **Custom Prompt…** — type any instruction on the spot

<Tip>
  Great on commit messages and PR descriptions — select a rough draft and ask
  for a tighter, clearer version.
</Tip>

## Options

Settings → Tools → AI Text Transform:

- **When done** — *replace the selection* or *copy to clipboard*.
