**Suggest a Tool** is how the Dax menu grows. If there's a small, sharp tool you
wish lived a keystroke away, tell us — the roadmap is shaped by what people
actually reach for.

## Open it

Choose **Suggest a Tool** from the Dax menu bar.

## Use it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the suggestion box">
    Choose Suggest a Tool from the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the tool">
    Tell us what it would do and when you'd use it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Submit — it goes straight to the team.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The most useful suggestions describe the moment: "when I'm doing X, I wish I
  could Y without leaving the app."
</Tip>

<Note>
  Have a bug or a note about an existing tool instead? Use [Feedback](/tools/feedback).
</Note>
