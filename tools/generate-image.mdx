**Generate Image** turns a text prompt into an image without opening a separate
app. Describe what you want, generate it, and use it — for a placeholder, an
icon, a diagram sketch, or a quick visual idea.

## Open it

Choose **Generate Image** from the Dax menu bar.

## Use it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe it">
    Type a prompt for the image you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Dax creates the image from your prompt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or share">
    Keep the result, or send it straight to [Image to URL](/tools/image-to-url)
    for a shareable link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Not sure how to phrase it? Start from [Image to Prompt](/tools/image-to-prompt)
  on a reference you like, then adjust the wording.
</Tip>

## Great for

- A placeholder or hero image while you build
- A quick icon or illustration
- Trying a visual idea before committing to it
