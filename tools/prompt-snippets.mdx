**Prompt Snippets** gives you a searchable palette of saved prompts — reusable
blocks you type or paste into any app with a keystroke. On-device only.

## Open it

Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>J</kbd> from any app.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the palette">
    Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>J</kbd>. Your saved snippets float
    up in a searchable list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search and pick">
    Type to filter, then press <kbd>↩</kbd> to use the selected snippet.
    <kbd>⇥</kbd> toggles between copying it to the clipboard and typing it
    directly into your focused app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage snippets">
    Add, edit, or remove snippets in **Settings → Tools → Prompt Snippets**.
    Dax ships with a set of built-in snippets to get you started.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips

- Snippets stay entirely on your Mac — nothing is sent to any server.
- Use snippets for system prompts, code review checklists, commit templates,
  or any block of text you reach for repeatedly.
- The <kbd>⇥</kbd> toggle remembers your last choice per session.
